# 产业动态

Use the 7 days ending at the frozen Asia/Shanghai task launch time. Search live webpages for consequential developments relevant to Chinese markets, covering several industries as the evidence warrants. Open original issuer announcements, exchange filings, official statistics, customer statements and industry data; use media to discover leads and corroborate events.

Prioritize measurable changes in demand, supply, orders, pricing, inventories and capacity. Separate a signed binding order from an intention, planned capacity from commissioned output, and a company projection from observed demand. Record the source period, units and comparison base for every central number. Do not infer revenue, profit or stock-price upside solely from a headline.

Group repeated coverage of one event. Rank at most 8 developments by the size of the change, relevance across the value chain and evidence strength, with a short explanation of why each matters. Include event time and publication time separately, the observed fact, affected value-chain link, confidence or unresolved evidence, and a clickable original source. Add one concrete next verification indicator per item.

Open with the actual cutoff and concise findings. If fewer material changes can be substantiated, deliver that smaller set. Mark unverified allegations and forecasts; do not present them as confirmed industrial changes. Report live-search failures or stale data openly. The output supports further research and does not initiate trading or formal screening.
